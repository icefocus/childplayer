# Children's Personal Information Protection Rules

App: Children Video Player. Provider: wangbailong. Contact: 9546459@qq.com. Effective date: 2026-10-26.

Guardians should read these rules with the Privacy Policy and select lawful, age-appropriate media. App Store age bands and the legal definition of children under 14 in mainland China are different classifications.

The app mainly processes imported files, folders, playback rules and usage on the device. It does not actively request a child's name, birthday, photograph, location, phone number or social profile. Videos may themselves contain images or voices of children. A guardian should review them and avoid importing another person's sensitive information without authorization.

Importing, deleting, renaming, moving, parent-only visibility and playback settings are controlled through guardian management. A temporary playback extension requires selecting a duration and verifying the parental PIN. Guardians should protect their PIN and restore parental control when finished. This is not a device-wide lock or absolute legal verification of guardian identity.

Baidu Netdisk is an optional source initiated by a guardian using an account they are entitled to use. Do not send children's cloud passwords to the developer. Baidu accounts, authorization pages, logs and downloads follow its applicable official policies. A parental gate does not replace informed guardian consent or third-party children's privacy duties. Guardians may choose to use local files only.

Processing personal information of children under 14 must follow applicable guardian-consent rules. Sensitive information, disclosures to third parties or cross-border processing may require further notices and consent based on the actual processing. Neither this text nor a PIN replaces those procedures.

Guardians can manage local files/settings, revoke Baidu authorization and contact 9546459@qq.com about their rights. We will not request a child's identity documents, cloud password or unrelated information to handle a request. There is currently no single button clearing every local authorization and historical record; see the Privacy Policy for details.

These rules take effect together with the Privacy Policy. Material changes will be notified as required by law. Public policies: https://github.com/icefocus/childplayer .
