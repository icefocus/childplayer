# Children Video Player Privacy Policy

Provider: wangbailong. Contact: 9546459@qq.com. Effective date: 2026-10-26.

## Scope
The app manages and plays videos supplied by you, with parental controls and optional Baidu Netdisk import. You do not need an app account. Guardians should read this policy and the Children's Personal Information Protection Rules before children use the app.

## Information processed on your device
Imported files, file names, folder structure, size/modification metadata, import tasks and progress are stored in the app's local library. Folder rules, daily playback usage, temporary extensions and necessary clock-validation state are processed locally. Language and appearance preferences are stored on the device. We do not operate a server receiving these local videos or playback records.

The parental PIN is represented by a salted derived verifier in the system Keychain. We do not send your plain-text parental PIN to the developer. A PIN controls actions within this app; it does not encrypt video files or control the whole device or computer file-sharing permissions.

## Optional Baidu Netdisk
A guardian can enter management mode and initiate official Baidu authorization. Baidu authenticates its own credentials; this app does not implement capture or storage of your plain-text Baidu password. Authorization tokens are received and stored in the device Keychain to request authorized account details, directory listings, file identifiers and downloads. Baidu and its download services receive tokens, requested paths/identifiers and network information necessary to supply the service, such as IP addresses and request logs. Baidu's processing is governed by the applicable official service policies.

The app restricts service requests to Baidu-related domains. The official sign-in page and cookies are handled by the system web component. Switching accounts uses an ephemeral browser session, which does not promise to remove all pre-existing cookies. The app supports switching accounts and renewed authorization, but currently has no single action clearing every local token and browser cookie. Revoke access through Baidu account authorization management and contact us about local residual data. Baidu: https://pan.baidu.com/ . Privacy center: https://privacy.baidu.com/ . You can use local playback without this optional service.

## Third parties and system services
We do not sell personal information, integrate advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs, or use app data for cross-app advertising tracking. The default integration does not use a developer-operated token relay. A future change to servers, SDKs or processing purposes will require updated disclosures and consent where applicable.

MobileVLCKit decodes compatible media on the device. Apple frameworks handle playback, file selection, web authorization and Keychain storage. User-selected file providers, computer file sharing, AirPlay and backups follow user actions and system settings. We do not promise that material deliberately shared or backed up can never leave the device.

## Permissions and choices
The app uses system document selection for files you choose and supports its Documents file-sharing area. It does not currently request camera, microphone, location, contacts or full photo-library access, and does not use advertising identifiers. Network access supports optional cloud authorization and downloads. Choosing not to use the cloud does not prevent playback of already imported content, subject to the guardian's playback rules.

## Retention, security and backups
Local media, indexes and rules remain with your library; there is no single automatic expiration period for all historical usage. Deleting a video does not necessarily erase usage history or account authorization. Keep original backups. Uninstalling usually removes the app sandbox, but Keychain items or system/computer backups may remain. Revoke third-party authorization and manage backups separately where needed.

We use the system sandbox, Keychain and HTTPS. No software can guarantee absolute security or prevent every device-level bypass. Relevant security incidents will be handled and notified as required by applicable law.

## Your rights
Guardians can view, rename, move or delete imported content and change playback/display settings through the protected management interface. To request access, correction, deletion, withdrawal or complaint handling, email 9546459@qq.com. We may verify necessary information, but will not request your cloud password or a complete identity-document photograph. We cannot remotely delete local files we do not hold.

## Children and updates
The app does not actively request a child's name, birthday, location or contact details. User videos and playback records may nevertheless contain children's information. Guardians must choose lawful, age-appropriate material. Network processing involving children under 14 must satisfy applicable guardian-consent and sensitive-data requirements. A four-digit PIN is not proof of every legally required notice or consent. Material processing changes will be disclosed as required.
